What is account verification?

Account verification helps keep spambots off Weasyl by requiring every new account to be vouched for by an established member of the community before features like commenting, writing journals, and uploading are available.

When should I verify someone else’s account?

When you’re confident they’re a real member of the community, not a scam, impersonator, or bot.

Scams and bots can be very convincing these days, so please don’t verify a stranger’s account just because it asked you to, followed you, or favorited your post. Please do verify an account of a friend you know from elsewhere!
